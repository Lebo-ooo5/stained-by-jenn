About Stained.By.Jenn

Jennifer Mabasa

Nail technician and founder

Welcome to Stained.By.Jenn, where beauty, creativity and individuality come together. Every client is treated with care and attention. Our goal is to create beautiful results while making every visit enjoyable and memorable.

Whether you want a fresh look, a special occasion style, or simply some time to treat yourself, we specialise in designing the most beautiful sets. We deliver quality, long-lasting nail sets, and we are a passionate beauty brand dedicated to helping our clients feel their best.

You will often find our work on Instagram and TikTok, where short videos show each set coming to life.

Our mission

To give clients beautifully designed, fashionable nails and beauty treatments in a friendly, knowledgeable and meticulous environment, and to show that handmade craftsmanship and personal care can never be replaced by mass production.

Our vision

To grow Stained.By.Jenn into one of Soweto's most sought-after nail studios, known for consistently high-quality, camera-ready nail art, exceptional service and keeping up with the latest beauty trends.

Who we serve

Young women in Soweto and greater Johannesburg who want dependable, fashionable nail treatments and follow beauty trends on social media.

Questions we get asked

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form, choose a design, date and time, and Jenn will confirm by email or WhatsApp. You can also message us on WhatsApp.

Can I book on a Sunday?

Sundays are by appointment only. Pick a Sunday on the booking form and Jenn will let you know if she can fit you in.

Can I ask for a design that is not in the gallery?

Yes. Describe the look in the notes box on the booking form, or send an inspiration photo on WhatsApp.

Where is the studio?

23 Thistle Street, Protea Glen, Soweto. See the map on the contact page for directions.
