About Stained.By.Jenn

Welcome to Stained.By.Jenn, where beauty, creativity, and individuality come together. Every client is treated with care and attention. Our goal is to create beautiful results while making every visit enjoyable and memorable.

Whether you are looking for a fresh look, a special occasion style, or simply some time to treat yourself, we specialize in creating and designing the most beautiful sets. We deliver quality and long-lasting nail sets. We are a passionate beauty brand dedicated to helping our clients feel their best.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide clients with exquisitely designed fashionable nails and beauty treatments in a friendly, knowledgeable, and meticulous environment.

Our Vision

To grow Stained.By.Jenn into one of Soweto's most sought-after nail studios, known for consistently high-quality, camera-ready nail art. We are committed to keeping up with the latest trends.

Target Audience

Stained.By.Jenn showcases young women in Soweto and the surrounding Johannesburg area, especially those who want dependable, fashionable nail treatments and follow beauty trends.
